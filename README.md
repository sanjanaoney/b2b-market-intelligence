# B2B Market Intelligence & Prospect Qualification

## Project Overview

This project explores potential B2B customers for a hypothetical company selling HR Management Software (HRMS) in Bangladesh.

The research focuses on 50 software and IT-related companies and evaluates how closely they match the client's target requirements.

## Research Criteria

Companies were assessed based on three criteria:

- Operations in Bangladesh
- Software development or software products as a core business
- Employee size between 51 and 500

Based on these criteria, companies were classified as Strong Fit, Considerable Fit, or Not Relevant.

## Key Findings

Out of 50 companies:

- **40 companies** were classified as Strong Fit.
- **8 companies** were classified as Considerable Fit.
- **2 records** were classified as Not Relevant under the screening model.

These classifications represent initial prospect screening, not confirmed customer interest.

## Excel Workbook

The workbook contains three worksheets:

- **Dashboard:** A summary of the research findings, classifications, and charts.
- **Company Directory:** Company profiles, business information, research findings, and client-fit assessments.
- **Methodology:** Research objectives, selection criteria, data sources, and analysis approach.

## Tools and Methods

- Microsoft Excel
- Secondary research
- Company profiling
- B2B prospect screening
- Excel formulas including COUNTIF, COUNTA, and IF

## Project Scope

The research uses publicly available company information and approximate employee-size ranges. It is intended to support preliminary market analysis and prospect identification rather than establish purchasing intent or sales readiness.
